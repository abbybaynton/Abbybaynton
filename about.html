About Abby

Short Bio: Abby Baynton is a leading book-marketing consultant who has generated over $20 million in author revenue through strategic campaigns. Her clients range from Big-5 publishers to debut indie authors.

Full Story

Abby started her career in New York publishing houses, steering publicity for HarperOne and later spearheading digital campaigns at Penguin Random House. After noticing the gap between traditional budgets and indie-author needs, she launched her boutique agency in 2014.

Since then she has:

  • Run 500+ Amazon, Facebook & BookBub ad campaigns
  • Secured 1,200+ media features
  • Grown an email community of 80k avid readers across 12 genre lists
  • In addition, i partner with a network of more than 20,000 book clubs throughout the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and beyond.

Abby’s approach marries narrative positioning with algorithmic precision every blurb, keyword and audience segment is A/B-tested for maximum ROI.
